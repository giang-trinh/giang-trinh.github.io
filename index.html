I am currently a researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology (VAST), since November 2026. From September 2025 to October 2026, I was a lecturer and researcher at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the LIFEWARE team at Inria Saclay from October 2024 to August 2025, and in the LIRICA team at LIS from April 2022 to September 2024. I received my PhD from the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology in November 2021.

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of theoretical computer science, mathematical logic, symbolic artificial intelligence, and discrete dynamical systems. I investigate the semantics, structural properties, and computational complexity of Boolean networks, logic programming, formal argumentation, and Petri nets. A central objective is to uncover and exploit connections among these formalisms to develop new theoretical foundations and symbolic computational methods. My interests also extend to computational systems biology and constraint-based optimization.
